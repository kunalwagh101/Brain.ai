# UAT — F-10.25 Team Channel Groups

Story: `S-10.25.01`

Status: `UAT_PENDING`

Use official WorkOS authentication and PostgreSQL with Owner/Admin, Team creator, channel creator, ordinary Member and restricted-channel member.

## Team → group → channel hierarchy

Create an active Team and two channel groups. Move authorised organisation and restricted channels into Team/group locations.

Expected: the sidebar renders Team → group → channel nesting. Channels assigned directly to a Team appear in **Ungrouped**. Channels with no active Team appear under global **Unassigned channels**.

## ACL invariance

Before moving a restricted channel, capture its NativeChannelMembership rows, ResourceGrant rows, visibility and evidence/Search access. Move it between Team/group locations and unassign it.

Expected: only `team_id` / `channel_group_id` change. Membership/grant/visibility/evidence/Search state is identical.

## Authority / tenant boundaries

As an ordinary restricted-channel reader, try to move the channel.

Expected: fail closed.

As a channel creator who does not manage the target Team, try to assign it there.

Expected: fail closed.

Try a group ID under the wrong Team or another organisation.

Expected: fail closed without hidden channel/group details.

## Archive fallback

Archive a group containing visible channels.

Expected: the group disappears from active nested navigation and its visible channels remain reachable under that Team's **Ungrouped** section. Restore it and confirm nesting returns.

Archive the Team.

Expected: its visible channels remain reachable under global **Unassigned channels**. No channel/permission row changes.

## Hidden restricted channels

As a user who cannot read a restricted channel, open the Team/group workspace.

Expected: Team/group metadata may be visible, but the hidden channel's ID/name/count is not exposed because hierarchy is composed only from the permission-filtered native-channel list.
